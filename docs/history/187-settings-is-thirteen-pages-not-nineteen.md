## Settings is thirteen pages, not nineteen

**Asked as "look at all of account settings. make it all more efficient and intuitive it looks long
right now."** A tutor's column was twelve `PROFILE_GROUPS` cards, a username card, a PIN card and
the wardrobe. Merged by what somebody thinks of as one question: `Yours` (one box) into About you,
`Where you are` into Where, `Studying now` and `More qualifications` into What you teach, and the
username and PIN onto one `Signing in` card with their own two buttons. `FIELD_ROWS` in me.js lays
pairs side by side when both are on the page — first/last name, city/town, borough/postcode, photo
and video links, each subject and its level, what you are studying and where, and the three
adjectives under one caption. Measured at 320x568: all thirteen pages fit their pane. Clients and
students lose the `Where you are` page the same way.
