## "Chance has a shape" makes the shape now

**Asked for as "refine chance has a shape animation. make it better."** The Galton splash was three
balls sliding straight through the pegs over five bars that pulsed together whatever landed — nothing
bounced and the bell was drawn before any ball arrived. Now sixteen balls drop one at a time, touch
the top of every peg, hop half a step, and pile up gold-then-teal in their bins; every one of the
sixteen left/right routes is taken once, so the pile is **1, 4, 6, 4, 1 by construction**, the counts
come up under the bins, it holds, and the board empties. `tools/galton.py` writes the circles and the
keyframes from the peg grid; each circle sits at its landing spot so reduced motion shows the finished
bell. Screenshotted at 1.5s, 4s and 8.6s.
