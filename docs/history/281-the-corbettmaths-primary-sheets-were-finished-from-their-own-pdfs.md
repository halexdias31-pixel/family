## The Corbettmaths primary sheets were finished from their own PDFs

The owner: *"you know how you still need me to give you the pngs of the corbettmaths stuff for sats.
cant you just convert the pdfs into pngs so that you can get the rest of the sats corbet maths stuff
done?"* — and yes. This machine cannot reach Google directly (the proxy answers 403), but the Drive
connector's download is spilled to a file by the harness when it is too big to return, intact; the
base64 decodes byte-exact (checked against Drive's size) and pymupdf renders the pages at 150 dpi.

**358 questions on 49 sheets had no answer**, and most had lost their numbers too: the transcription
read the PDF's text layer, which holds none of the artwork — "Work out the perimeter of this
rectangle cm", with no rectangle and no lengths. A workflow of ten transcribers (about five sheets
each) read every page, restored the words, drew 272 diagrams in the house style, and wrote answers
and accept lists; a verifier per group re-read the page and re-solved every row before anything was
kept. 1,769 field changes and 5 rows the transcription had skipped entirely, applied line by line.

**There are no Corbettmaths answer sheets in Drive** — the "Answers_-_*.pdf" files are a different
publisher's (Maths4Everyone) — so every answer was worked from the question and checked by a second
agent, not copied.

**Found on the way:** Similar Shapes and Translations were filed against 1st Class Maths GCSE PDFs and
now point at their Corbettmaths sheets; "Area of a Triangle" rows 13–20 are questions 13–20 of a
different sheet ("Area of a Square / Area of a Rectangle"), answered from that sheet's PDF but still
filed under the triangle paper — moving them, and adding that sheet's questions 1–12, is a new paper
rather than a patch. Ten drawing descriptions came out over check-answers' 120 characters and were
cut by hand; the 677-digit cube number's ACCEPTED line came off because its head is now short.
