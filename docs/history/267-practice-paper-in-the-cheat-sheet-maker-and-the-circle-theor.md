## Practice paper in the cheat sheet maker, and the circle-theorem splash redrawn

**Asked for as** *"allow cheat sheet maker to make a handwriting worksheet. or lined or grid paper."* and *"refine the circle theorems animation"*.

### Practice paper (js/mat.js, style.css)

- The paper kinds are a **Practice paper** group at the foot of the **subject** select: Handwriting, Lined paper, Squared paper. The first build put them in a "Sheet type" select of their own, and that 44px row made `check/states.js` fail its 320x568 fit walk, which had no row to spare. Choosing a paper kind hides the level, the topic and the pieces (`#mat-cheat`), with their ticks kept. Choosing a subject brings the cheat sheet back.
- **Handwriting** uses the standard four-line ruling. It has three equal bands: a faint ascender line, a dashed x-height line, a dark baseline and a faint descender line. A skip of 1.5 bands separates one ruling from the next. With a one-band skip, the first screenshot read as one continuous set of lines. The learner's words are drawn as pale letters with a dotted edge on the trace rows. One or two empty practice rows follow each trace row, and the words repeat until the page is full. Sizes are 8, 6 and 4mm bands. Wrapping is balanced, so a last word is never left alone on a ruling. The font size is the band divided by the face's **measured** x-height (`1ex` in a probe), so a lower-case `a` fills the middle band in whatever face the device prints with. `font-size-adjust` was tried first, and Chrome drew SVG text 3.8× too big with it.
- **Lined** paper is ruled at 8, 6, 10 or 12mm, with a 25mm margin. **Squared** paper comes in 5mm, 7mm or 1cm squares, whole squares only, centred.
- All rulings are SVG strokes measured in millimetres, so they print. The drawing is 184 × 260mm, a height measured in Chrome to leave 3mm above the footer. Every colour is a `--pa-` token.
- Each setting comes from a closed list (`MAT_BLANK_OPTS`) through `matBlankSet_`. A stored value the selects do not offer is refused, and an unknown stored kind opens the cheat sheet.
- Printing uses the same path (`MAT_SHEET` → `mat-print`). The result is one A4 page, confirmed by printing to PDF. The basket gets the same `print` line, so the laminate switch comes with it. Its parts carry the words and the sizes, so the owner can rebuild the sheet.
- Check: a new journey in `check-flow.js`, *"the cheat sheet maker draws a handwriting sheet, lined and squared paper, and prints them"*. Ten mutations were tried, and each one turned it red for the right reason.

### The angle at the centre (`#splash-cent`, now generated by `tools/cent.py`)

- It was a flip-book of three fixed triangles with no angle marked. Now A, B and the centre angle 2x stay still, and P slides round the major arc. It holds at each end and the loop is 8s. The angle at P is **one wedge carried round and turned**, so you can see that its size does not change. The arc AB both angles stand on is picked out. The caption names the theorem: "the angle at the centre / is twice the angle at the edge".
- P's travel is kept to 228–312°. The first draw went to 205°, and there one chord lay along a radius.
- Each chord is drawn 100 units long and shrunk to fit. A unit line stretched about 60× smeared its antialiasing past B, because Chrome scales the composited bitmap.
- Only transforms move, on one clock with no seam. Under reduced motion the splash shows P at the top. The colours are scoped (`--ct-cen`, `--ct-edge`), and the rim is `--paper` at an opacity rather than the old rgb literal.
- Check: `check-splash-loops.js` now covers `cent`. It re-derives P from the keyframes at every stop and halfway between, and checks that P is on the rim and that the two chords and the wedge meet. It also checks that the wedge opens half the centre angle and points at M, that the label keeps its distance, and that the caption names the theorem. Seven mutations were tried, and every one turned the check red.

**For the owner to confirm:** the handwriting face is whatever the device has, from Comic Sans MS, Comic Neue, Andika, Century Gothic, Trebuchet MS, then sans-serif. Nothing is downloaded. Paper sheets go in the basket as ordinary one-page print lines.
