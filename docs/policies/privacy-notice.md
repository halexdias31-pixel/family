# Privacy notice

## Who we are

## What we collect
- **Parents and adult learners:** name, email, phone, home address (for home visits), what you book and pay, messages to us.
- **Children:** first name, school year, subjects and levels, what they need help with, lesson notes and work, and any
  health, special educational need or access information you choose to tell us so lessons are safe and suitable.
- **Tutors and applicants:** full name, contact details, home address, date of birth, ID and right-to-work document,
  qualification copies, referees' details and references, DBS certificate number and Update Service status, subjects, availability,
  rate, training records, bank details for payment, and the tutor agreement you signed. Stored on the business's Google account, seen only by the owner.
- **Using the site:** sign-in records and basic technical logs needed to keep it working and secure. No advertising cookies.

## Why we use it, and the lawful basis
| Purpose | Lawful basis (UK GDPR Art. 6) |
|---|---|
| Arranging, teaching and charging for lessons | Contract |
| Tax and payment records (HMRC requires them) | Legal obligation |
| Safeguarding children; DBS and recruitment checks | Legal obligation; legitimate interests (keeping children safe) |
| Lesson notes, progress and reminders | Legitimate interests (teaching well) |
| Occasional news about new courses (stop any time) | Consent, or the soft opt-in for existing customers |

Health and SEN information is special category data: we use it only with your explicit consent or where needed to
safeguard a child (UK GDPR Art. 9; Data Protection Act 2018, Sch. 1). DBS information is criminal offence data, handled under the same Schedule.

## Children
We collect only what lessons need (see [Children's data](childrens-data.md)). A parent books for a child and gives consent
for any online account for a child under 13. Young people aged 13 and over can ask us about their own data.

## Who we share it with
We never sell data. We share only what each needs:
- **Stripe** — takes card payments; we never see or store your full card number.
- **Google** (Workspace, Apps Script) — stores our records and email on the business's account.
- **GitHub Pages** — hosts the website; no personal data is published there.
- **Your child's tutor** — only what they need to teach.
- **Authorities** — police, the local authority (MASH or LADO), the DBS or HMRC where the law requires, or to protect a child.

Some providers store data outside the UK; they do so under UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

## How long we keep it
| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Bookings, receipts, payments | 6 years after the end of the tax year |
| Lesson notes and children's work | 1 year after lessons end |
| Safeguarding records | Until the child is 25 (longer if the local authority asks) |
| Tutor records | While the tutor works with us, then 6 years; DBS: number, date and result only |
| Unsuccessful applicants and enquiries | 6 months |

You can ask to see your data (we answer within one month), correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, move it,
and withdraw consent at any time. Some rights have limits (we must keep tax records, for example). Ask: [email].
