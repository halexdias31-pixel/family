# Safeguarding policy

*Adopted: November 2026. Review: every November, and after any serious incident or change in the law.*
Based on the DfE's *Keeping children safe in out-of-school settings* (guidance for providers, including tuition),
*Working Together to Safeguard Children*, *Keeping Children Safe in Education*, and the NSPCC safeguarding policy template.

## Our commitment
Every child we teach has an equal right to be safe from harm, and the child's welfare always comes first. This policy applies
to the owner, every tutor and anyone working for @family., in homes, online and anywhere else.

The DSL holds DSL-level training, refreshed every two years. Every tutor completes safeguarding training before teaching and every year.

## Recognising concerns
Abuse can be physical, emotional, sexual or neglect, and includes exploitation, online abuse, radicalisation, FGM, domestic abuse and
child-on-child abuse. Signs include unexplained injuries, sudden changes in behaviour, fear of an adult, or a child saying something worrying.

## If a child tells you something
Listen and stay calm. Do not ask leading questions or promise secrecy ("I have to tell someone who can help").
Write down what was said in the child's words, with date, time and who was there. **Tell the DSL the same day.**

The DSL decides on a referral and makes it within 24 hours. Anyone may refer directly if the DSL is unavailable or does not act.
Parents are told unless that could put the child at greater risk; the MASH advises.

## Allegations against the owner or a tutor
Any sign that an adult has harmed a child, may have committed an offence against a child, or has behaved in a way that shows they
may be unsuitable to work with children goes to the **LADO within one working day**, before we look into it ourselves:
- Merton LADO: 020 8545 3179 / 3187, lado@merton.gov.uk
- Wandsworth LADO: through the MASH, 020 8871 6622, lado@wandsworth.gov.uk

If it concerns the DSL, the deputy (or whoever hears it) goes straight to the LADO. The adult is not told details until the LADO agrees,
and does not teach while it is looked into if the LADO advises. If someone is removed from work with children because they harmed or
posed a risk to a child, we must refer them to the **DBS**. Low-level concerns are written down and reviewed by the DSL.

## Safe working rules
- The [home visits](home-visits.md) and [online safety](online-safety.md) rules are part of this policy.
- No contact with children on personal phones or social media; messages go through the site or to the parent.
- No gifts, favours or secrets; no physical contact beyond what is necessary and appropriate.
- Every tutor is recruited under the [safer recruitment](safer-recruitment.md) policy, with an enhanced DBS and children's barred list check.

## Records
Concerns are written up the same day, factually, signed and dated, and kept securely apart from lesson records; only the DSL sees them.
Kept until the child is 25. Allegations against adults: until their normal retirement age or 10 years, whichever is longer.

## Review
The DSL reviews this policy every November, checks every contact number still works, and ticks the review on the business checklist.
