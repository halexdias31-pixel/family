# @family. — policies

Plain-English policies for @family., a small home and online tutoring business in Merton and Wandsworth, London.
They are for families, tutors and the owner. Each is reviewed at least once a year (see the date at the top of each).
Guidance written for the business, not legal advice.

| For | Document | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Families | [Terms for families](family-terms.md) | What we provide, prices, paying, term bookings, ending |
| Families | [Cancellation and refunds](cancellation-refunds.md) | 14-day right to cancel, notice, missed lessons, refunds |
| Families | [Complaints](complaints.md) | How to complain and how fast we answer |
| Everyone | [Privacy notice](privacy-notice.md) | What personal data we hold, why, and your rights |
| Everyone | [Children's data](childrens-data.md) | The extra care we take with children's information |
| Everyone | [Safeguarding policy](safeguarding-policy.md) | Keeping children safe; who to tell and how |

| Everyone | [Home visits](home-visits.md) | Rules for lessons in a family's home |
| Owner | [Data breach plan](data-breach-plan.md) | What to do if data is lost or seen by the wrong person |

## Placeholders the owner must fill

Search these files for `[` to find each one: `[DSL name]`, `[deputy DSL name]`, `[phone]`, `[email]`, `[address]`,
`[ICO registration number]`, `[insurer]`, and the LADO and MASH contacts in the safeguarding policy.
This repository is public: fill real names, emails and numbers in the copy you publish or print, not here.

## Where the site links them

The booking form (under its tiles) links the family documents and the Staff hub's tutor terms card links the tutor ones.
The base address is the brand key `policies_url`; set it empty to hide the links.
