# Complaints

*Last reviewed: November 2026.*

We want to put things right quickly. **A worry about a child's safety is not a complaint**: tell the DSL at once
(see the [safeguarding policy](safeguarding-policy.md)), or call 999 in an emergency.

## How to complain
1. **Talk to us.** Tell your tutor or the owner: [email], [phone]. Most things are sorted here.
2. **Put it in writing.** If not, email [email]: what happened, when, and what you would like done.
   We acknowledge within **3 working days** and answer in full within **14 days**, or tell you why we need longer.
3. **Review.** If you are unhappy with the answer, ask for a review. The owner looks at it afresh and gives a final answer within 14 days.

## If you are still unhappy
- About personal data: the ICO (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).
- Consumer advice: the Citizens Advice consumer helpline, 0808 223 1133.
- You can also use the small claims court.

## What we keep
A short record of each complaint, what we did and the outcome, kept for 6 years and used to improve the service.
