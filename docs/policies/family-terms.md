# Terms for families

*Version: November 2026.* The contract between you (the parent, carer or adult learner who books) and @family.
Read with our [cancellation and refunds](cancellation-refunds.md), [privacy notice](privacy-notice.md) and
[safeguarding policy](safeguarding-policy.md).

## 1. What we provide
One-to-one and small-group tutoring for ages about 5 to 18, and adults, in your home (Merton and Wandsworth) or online by video.
Lessons are taught by the owner or a tutor who has passed our [safer recruitment](safer-recruitment.md) checks.
We teach with reasonable care and skill; we cannot promise a particular grade or exam result.

## 2. Prices
Prices are as shown on the site when you book, including any travel charge or shared-lesson split. The price on your booking is what
you pay for that booking. We give at least 4 weeks' notice of price changes for future terms.

## 3. Booking and payment
- You book online, by the **term** (or single lessons where offered). The contract starts when we confirm the booking.
- You pay **by card at booking**, through Stripe. We never store your card details. Your receipt is on the site.
- If a payment fails, we may hold the booking until it is paid.

## 4. Your part
Be ready at the booked time, and have a parent or carer at home (in the room for under-8s); see [home visits](home-visits.md)
and [online safety](online-safety.md). Tell us anything that affects your child's safety or learning.

## 5. Changes and missed lessons
See [cancellation and refunds](cancellation-refunds.md). If we must cancel, we offer another time or refund that lesson.
If your usual tutor is unavailable, we may offer another checked tutor.

## 6. Ending
- You can cancel within 14 days of booking, and end a term booking later with notice, as the cancellation policy sets out.
- We may end the agreement if fees are unpaid, or if behaviour puts a tutor or child at risk, refunding any lessons not taught.

## 7. Our responsibility
We are responsible for foreseeable loss caused by our breaking these terms or not using reasonable care. Nothing here limits your rights
under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, or our liability for death or injury caused by negligence.
