# Home visits

*Last reviewed: November 2026.* Part of the [safeguarding policy](safeguarding-policy.md).

## Rules for every home lesson
- **A parent or carer stays at home for the whole lesson.** If no adult is home, the tutor does not start and tells the owner.
- **Under-8s: a parent or carer is in the same room** for the whole lesson.
- Lessons happen in a shared room (kitchen, dining or living room) with the door open, never a bedroom.
- **Tutors never take a child anywhere else**: not out of the house, to the tutor's home, a car, a shop or a park. No lifts.
- The tutor arrives and leaves at the booked times and hands the child back to the parent at the end.
- No photos of the child or the home; no personal contact details swapped with the child.
- Tutors carry photo ID and their DBS details and show them when asked.

## Before the first visit
The parent confirms the address, who will be home, pets, allergies or access needs, and an emergency contact.
The owner passes the tutor only what they need.

## Tutor safety
Tutors share their lesson times with the owner. A tutor who feels unsafe leaves and tells the owner.
Any accident, injury or worry is written down and reported to the owner the same day, and to the DSL for any concern about a child.
