# Online safety (video lessons)

*Last reviewed: November 2026.* Part of the [safeguarding policy](safeguarding-policy.md).

## For families
- A parent agrees to online lessons at booking; for a child under 13 a parent sets up access.
- A parent or carer is at home and within earshot for the whole lesson; for under-8s, in the room.
- The child joins from a shared space, not a bedroom.
- Lessons use only the link the business sends, on the business's account.

## For tutors
- Use only the business's video account and the lesson link; use the waiting room and lock the meeting once everyone is in.
- Plain background, professional dress, no one else in your room, headphones if others are nearby.
- No one-to-one contact with a child outside the lesson: no private chat, calls, texts, social media or gaming.
  Messages go through the site or to the parent.
- Do not record a lesson unless a parent agrees in writing for a stated reason; keep it on the business's account and delete it within 30 days.
- No screenshots or photos of the child. Share only learning materials; close other tabs and notifications first.
- If something worrying happens on screen or a child says something worrying, end the lesson calmly if needed and tell the DSL the same day.

## For everyone
Report online sexual abuse to CEOP (ceop.police.uk). Family advice: nspcc.org.uk, "Online safety".
