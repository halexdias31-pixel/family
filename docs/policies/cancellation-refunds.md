# Cancellation and refunds

*Version: November 2026.*

## Your 14-day right to cancel
A booking made online is a distance contract under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges)
Regulations 2013. You can cancel **for any reason within 14 days** of the day we confirm your booking.
- Tell us by email ([email]) or on the site. A clear statement is enough; you may use the model form below.
- We refund within **14 days** of hearing from you, to the card you paid with.
- **If you asked for lessons to start within the 14 days**, we deduct a fair amount for lessons already taught.
  Once every booked lesson has been taught, the right to cancel ends.

> Model cancellation form — To @family., [email]: I cancel my booking for [lessons], made on [date]. Name: … Address: … Date: …

## After 14 days: ending a term booking
- Give **2 weeks' notice** by email or on the site. We refund every lesson booked after the notice ends.
- Lessons within the notice period are paid for, unless we can fill the slot.

## Cancelling one lesson
| When you tell us | What happens |
|---|---|
| 24 hours or more before | Moved to another time this term, or credited |
| Less than 24 hours before, or no one home | Charged in full; we will try to rearrange if the tutor can |
| Illness on the day | One free rearrangement per term |

## If we cancel
We offer another time or refund that lesson in full. If we end a booking, we refund every untaught lesson.

## Refunds
Always to the card you paid with, through Stripe, within 14 days of agreeing them. Your statutory rights are not affected.
