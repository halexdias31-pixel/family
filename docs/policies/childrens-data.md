# Children's data

*Last reviewed: November 2026.* Follows UK GDPR and the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code).

## Collect only what lessons need
- **Yes:** first name, school year, subjects, level, what they find hard, lesson notes and work.
- **Only if a parent chooses to tell us**, to keep lessons safe and suitable: health, allergies, SEN or access needs.
- **No:** date of birth (school year is enough), photos, the child's own contact details, the school name unless an exam board
  depends on it, or anything "just in case".

## Consent and who decides
- **Under 13:** a parent or carer books, agrees the terms and gives consent for any online account, video lesson or app the
  child uses (13 is the UK age of consent for online services, UK GDPR Art. 8). We check the consent comes from the account holder.
- **13 and over:** bookings still go through a parent, but the young person can ask about their own data and we explain it to them directly.
- Consent can be withdrawn at any time; we then stop and delete what we no longer need.

## How we look after it
- Private by default: children's profiles are not public, share no location, and no one outside their lessons can message them.
- Only the child's own tutor and the owner see their notes.
- No profiling, no advertising, no selling.
- Tutors keep no children's data on personal phones or accounts; work stays on the business's Google account.
- Photos or recordings of a child only with a parent's written consent for a stated purpose, never published without it.

## Deleting it
Lesson notes and work are deleted 1 year after lessons end. Safeguarding records follow the [safeguarding policy](safeguarding-policy.md).
A lost or wrongly shared record follows the [data breach plan](data-breach-plan.md).
