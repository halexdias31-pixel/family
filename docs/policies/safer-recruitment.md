# Safer recruitment

*Last reviewed: November 2026.* Follows the DfE's out-of-school settings guidance and the safer recruitment part of
*Keeping Children Safe in Education*. No tutor is alone with any child until every check below is complete and recorded.

## What the tutor sends us first
Full name, contact details, home address, date of birth, right-to-work document, photo ID, copies of qualifications,
two referees' contact details, enhanced DBS certificate number with consent to an Update Service status check, subjects and levels,
availability and rate. Stored on the business's Google account only, seen only by the owner, kept as the [privacy notice](privacy-notice.md) says.

## The checks

| Check | How |
|---|---|
| **Application** | Full work and education history; any gaps explained at interview |
| **Interview** | Includes safeguarding questions and why they want to work with children |
| **Identity** | Original photo ID (passport or driving licence) and proof of address, seen in person |
| **Right to work** | Original document or a Home Office share code checked on gov.uk; keep a dated copy |
| **Qualifications** | Original certificates for the subjects and levels they will teach |
| **Two references** | Asked for **directly** from each referee, never passed on by the candidate; one from the latest employer or work with children; confirmed by phone |
| **Enhanced DBS** | Enhanced check **with the children's barred list**, through a registered umbrella body. If they are on the **DBS Update Service**, see the original certificate and do a status check with consent |
| **Time abroad** | Overseas criminal record check or certificate of good conduct for any country lived in during the last 5 years |
| **Safeguarding training** | Completed before the first lesson; renewed every year |
| **First lesson checked** | Good practice, not a legal requirement: the owner observes the first lesson, or sees a short demo lesson at interview, or checks in with the family afterwards |

## Decisions
Any concern from a reference, DBS result or gap is discussed with the candidate and decided by the owner, with reasons written down.
We record the DBS certificate number, date and result; we do not keep copies of certificates beyond 6 months.

## Ongoing
A yearly Update Service status check and safeguarding refresher, and a duty on every tutor to tell us at once of any caution,
conviction or investigation.
