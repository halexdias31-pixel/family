# Tutor onboarding

*Last reviewed: November 2026.* Every new tutor, in this order. No child is taught until steps 1 to 6 are done.

## What you send us
Your full name, phone and email, home address, date of birth, right-to-work document, photo ID, copies of your qualifications,
contact details for two referees (we contact them ourselves), your enhanced DBS certificate number and your consent for an
Update Service status check, the subjects and levels you teach, your availability, and your hourly rate.

**How it is stored:** on the business's Google account only, seen only by the owner, never in this public website's files.
We keep it while you work with us and 6 years after (6 months if you do not join); DBS: number, date and result only.
See the [privacy notice](privacy-notice.md).

## The steps
1. **Apply and interview** — the [safer recruitment](safer-recruitment.md) checks start: ID, right to work, qualifications, two references.
2. **Enhanced DBS** with the children's barred list, or an Update Service status check — cleared before teaching.
3. **Confirm self-employed status** with HMRC's Check Employment Status for Tax tool; keep the result.
4. **Sign the tutor agreement** on the site (Staff, Tutor terms); the version and date are recorded.
5. **Read and confirm** the [safeguarding policy](safeguarding-policy.md), [online safety](online-safety.md),
   [home visits](home-visits.md), [children's data](childrens-data.md) and the [data breach plan](data-breach-plan.md).
6. **Safeguarding training** — an accredited course (for example the NSPCC's online course); certificate kept, refreshed yearly.
7. **Set up** — access to the business's accounts only (no children's data on personal devices), lesson links, payment details.
8. **First lesson checked (good practice, not a legal requirement)** — one of: the owner observes the first lesson, or the
   tutor gives the owner a short demo lesson at interview, or the owner checks in with the family after the first lesson. A short note is kept.
9. **Check-in after the first half-term** — how it is going and any support needed.

## Records kept
The date of each step, DBS certificate number and date, training certificate, references, signed agreement and observation note.
Kept while the tutor works with us, then 6 years.
