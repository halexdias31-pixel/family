Docket screen

Use Chrome on a Chromebook or computer (not a phone). The screen has two buttons: ☀ and ⏻.

1 · Install

Unplug the screen. Hold ☀, plug it in while holding ☀, let go. Press Install and choose the screen.

2 · Your Wi-Fi and Docket

Unplug the screen and plug it back in. Then fill these in and press Send. They go only to the screen, over the cable; this page does not keep them.

The private address is on the Docket (the site, Docket, private address). To change Wi-Fi later, plug the screen in, press ⏻ once and do step 2 again. Free software (GPL-3.0); the source is in tools/docket-screen/.
