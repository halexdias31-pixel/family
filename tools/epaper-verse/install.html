Verse of the day

Puts the verse-a-day program on the Waveshare ESP32-S3-ePaper-1.54. Use Chrome (a Chromebook is fine).

  1. Plug the board into the Chromebook with a USB-C cable.
  2. Press Install below, choose the board in the list (it may be called "USB JTAG/serial debug unit"), press Connect, then Install. It takes about a minute.
  3. If it is not in the list or says it cannot connect: unplug it, hold the BOOT button, plug it back in, let go, and try again. Afterwards press RESET once.
  4. The screen then says Wi-Fi setup. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network Verse-Setup. A page opens (if not, open 192.168.4.1 in the phone's browser). Tap Configure WiFi, pick your home Wi-Fi, type its password, leave the verse address as it is, and press Save.
  5. A few seconds later today's verse appears. It changes every morning at about 6am.
This browser cannot talk to the board. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer or Chromebook. Open this page from its https:// address.

To change the Wi-Fi later: press RESET, then within two seconds press and hold BOOT until the setup screen shows. Free software: the program is GPL-3.0 and its source is in this site's repository, tools/epaper-verse/; see NOTICE.txt.
